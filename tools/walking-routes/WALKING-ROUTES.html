Routes on foot from Cave Junction to 359 cities — every incorporated city in Oregon, and California cities from the state line south to Walnut Creek. Each city has two routes, printable turn-by-turn directions, and a GPX track for GPS apps (OsmAnd, Avenza, Gaia) that works with no signal. Click a city for details.

  • Back roads — county, Forest Service and BLM roads and trails; stays off freeways. Quieter and less exposed, but often longer, steeper and more remote.
  • Highway — follows the main highways including I-5, US-199 and US-101. Usually the shortest, with gentler grades and real bridges, but exposed and where other people will be.
Days assume 15 miles a day carrying a pack (3 mph of steady walking). Mountains, heat, injuries and heavy loads slow you down. Routes come from OpenStreetMap data (Nov 2026) and know nothing about washed-out bridges, landslides, snow, fire, water or food. Starting elsewhere in the valley adds or subtracts a few miles.
