Routes from Cave Junction

On foot, by bicycle and by car to 359 towns: every incorporated city in Oregon, and California cities from the state line south to Walnut Creek. Each has turn-by-turn directions to print, and a GPS track that works with no signal.

How the times are worked out

On foot: 15 miles a day carrying a pack (3 miles an hour of steady walking). Back roads keep to county, Forest Service and BLM roads and trails, off the freeways: quieter and less exposed, but often longer, steeper, more remote, and snowy on high passes. The highway way follows the main highways, I-5, US-199 and US-101 among them: usually shorter, with gentler grades and real bridges, but exposed, and where other people will be.

By bicycle: riding time with a load at about 6 miles an hour on a route for bicycles. Add about an hour for every 1,000 feet of climbing, and time for rests and repairs. By car: the normal driving time with the roads open. After a disaster, plan without a car.

Mountains, heat, injuries and heavy loads slow you down. The routes come from OpenStreetMap (November 2026) and know nothing of washed-out bridges, landslides, snow, fire, water or food. Starting elsewhere in the valley adds or takes off a few miles.
