Switch on what you want to see. Tap anything on the map to find out what it is.

Property lines and owners are from the county assessor's tax-lot map. The lines can be off by many feet and are not a survey: don't use them to settle where a boundary is. Owners are as recorded when this map was made. Lot lines, owners and house numbers stop at the county line; some other layers stop at the state line.

Tap the map to mark points along the way. Two points also tell you whether one can see the other (for radio).

Your own marks: a spring you found, a cache, a berry patch. They are kept on this phone or computer only. To add one, tap the map and choose Save as a pin.
