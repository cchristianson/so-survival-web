# SO Survival Library

**Live site:** https://cchristianson.github.io/so-survival-web/

A free survival and self-reliance library for the Illinois Valley and southern Oregon that
**works offline in any modern browser** — phone, tablet or computer:

- **Ask** a question: the library is searched on your device and a small AI running **inside
  your browser** (WebLLM) writes an answer with numbered, clickable sources.
- **Library:** ~500 manuals and guides (maps, hunting/fishing/trapping, gardening & canning,
  water, animals & vet care, off-grid power & creek hydro, homestead skills, household
  make-do, wildfire, earthquake, radio, medical, medicines).
- **Guides & tools:** Illinois Valley quick reference, Make-Do household guide, found-medicine
  safety, an offline **pill identifier**, and **walking routes** from Cave Junction to 359
  Oregon and Northern California cities with GPS tracks.
- **Offline:** "⚙️ Offline → Save library" stores everything on the device; add it to your home
  screen. The AI model (about 0.9 GB or 2.3 GB) is saved after one download.

This site is generated by `tools/build-web.py` in the (private) project that also builds the
full offline drive edition. Don't edit files here by hand — they are replaced on each build.
