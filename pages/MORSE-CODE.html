Good to know

  • The timing is the code: a dash lasts three dots. Leave one dot of silence inside a letter, three between letters and seven between words.
  • SOS is sent as one unbroken run, ...---..., over and over. Any signal sent three times (three flashes, three blasts) also means help.
  • Reading a signal works best with a steady beep (a radio's Morse tone, a whistle, a horn) in a quiet place, or a clear light in the dark, at up to about 15 words a minute. A few words sent at an even pace read best; set the speed above to roughly match a very short message.
  • At night, 💡 Flash the screen turns the whole phone into a signal lamp: turn the brightness all the way up and point it.
