💾 Get the whole library on a USB drive

The full library adds every book, all of Wikipedia, maps that work with no signal, and an AI that needs no internet. No commands to type:

  1. Download the kit and unzip it.
  2. Double-click INSTALL-Windows (or INSTALL-Mac).
  3. In the page that opens, pick your drive and press Start.

⏸️ You can stop it any time and carry on later. Run it again, pick the same drive and press Continue: it picks up where it left off and fetches anything that failed.

🧰 ARK Vault: the whole library in an ammo can

A steel ammo can that holds this library on a small computer (a Raspberry Pi), a battery, the chargers to fill it from the sun or the wall, a little printer, a crank radio and an old phone to read it on. Sealed, it's shielded from an EMP. Open the lid and flip the power switch on, and the phone (or any phone nearby) uses the library and the AI over the Vault's own Wi-Fi, with no internet and no power grid.
