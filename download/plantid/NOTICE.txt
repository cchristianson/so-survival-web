The plant and mushroom identifier
=================================

Picture model: BioCLIP (imageomics/bioclip, https://huggingface.co/imageomics/bioclip), by the
Imageomics Institute, released under the MIT License (see the model card). Only its image half is
used, repacked with smaller numbers (int8) by tools/maintenance/plantid_build.py in this library.
  Stevens et al., "BioCLIP: A Vision Foundation Model for the Tree of Life", CVPR 2024.

in this area, with their common names and family trees (tools/maintenance/plantid_species.py).
